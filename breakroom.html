Kevin's Famous Chili

The legendary recipe from Kevin Malone.

Ingredients

  • 4 dried ancho chiles
  • 2 Tbsp oil
  • 3 lbs ground beef
  • 2 yellow onions, chopped
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 1 jalapeño, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp oregano
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 (12-oz) bottles of beer
  • 3 cans beans, drained
  • 3 cups beef stock
  • Chopped tomatoes & salt

Directions

  1. Toast and soak the ancho chiles, then blend into a paste.
  2. Brown the ground beef and set aside.
  3. Cook the onions (undercook them!), jalapeño, and garlic.
  4. Add spices, beer, chile paste, beans, stock, tomatoes, and beef.
  5. Simmer for 1.5–2 hours. Serve with cheese and sour cream.
  6. Try not to drop the pot.

Remember: undercook the onions.
