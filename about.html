Our Story

At Dunder Mifflin, we take paper seriously, even if we don't always take ourselves seriously. Our Scranton branch is home to a dedicated team of professionals who keep the office running (most of the time). Under the leadership of Michael Scott, we strive for customer satisfaction, workplace excellence, and the occasional mandatory team-building exercise.

We're not just a paper company. We're a family. A slightly dysfunctional one, but a family nonetheless.
